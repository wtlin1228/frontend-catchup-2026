Pattern 12 of 23: motion

Scripted motion

The motion that needs a scheduler: items that slide to new positions when a list reorders (FLIP, with the Web Animations API), items that animate in and out, a card you can throw that springs back, and a layout change animated by the View Transitions API. Frameworks differ a lot here: Svelte and Vue ship it, React needs a library.
