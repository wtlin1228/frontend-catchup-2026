Scroll-driven reveal

Scroll down. Each item fades and rises as it enters the viewport; the bar at the top of the page tracks reading progress. Both use animation-timeline.

  1. Ask what the motion is for. Confirmation, orientation, or decoration.
  2. Prefer transforms and opacity: they skip layout and paint.
  3. Animate from a declared starting state, not from "whatever it was".
  4. Keep durations short: 150 to 350 ms for interface motion.
  5. Give every animation a reduced-motion fallback.
  6. Let the platform drive it when it can: scroll, view and transition timelines.
  7. Measure on a slow device, not on the machine it was built on.
  8. Remove one animation before shipping.
