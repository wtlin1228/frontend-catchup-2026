Pattern 21 of 23: observability

A trace, end to end

One click loads a post and its related posts, then renders. Each step becomes a span; the server adds its own time through a Server-Timing header, which the browser also exposes on resource timing entries. The result is a waterfall you can export in an OpenTelemetry-shaped JSON. SvelteKit, Next.js and React's profiler each do a part of this for you.
