Pattern 15 of 23: state across navigation

Tabs that remember

Three tabs with state worth keeping: a scrolled list, a half-written form and a running animation. In keep-alive mode a hidden tab stays in the DOM, hidden and inert, with its animation paused. In destroy mode each switch rebuilds the tab, and the counters show what that costs. React's Activity, Vue's KeepAlive and Angular's route reuse are the framework versions of the first mode.
